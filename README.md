# Person-Centered Technology Impact Protocol

v0.1, not yet validated, feedback invited.

This is a draft protocol, not a validated measure. It is not a claim of originality. Do not use the priority as a probability, a welfare score, or proof that a product is safe.

Licensed under the [MIT License](LICENSE). That is permission to copy and adapt this draft. It is not a validation.

The question for one product and one person:

Who benefits, who bears the costs, through which systems, compared with what alternative — and what must change?

Each impact is scored on its own.

`priority = 100 × (magnitude × likelihood) / 16`

Magnitude is 0–4. Likelihood is 1–4, within a named horizon. The result is a queue score. Benefits and harms are never subtracted. Headcount is recorded and not multiplied. A blank rating stays blank. A harm at magnitude 3 or 4 goes to independent review no matter how large the benefits are.

The hypothetical sheet in the app is an illustration, not evidence.
