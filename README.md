# Person-Centered Technology Impact Protocol

Version 0.1. Not validated. Not a claim of originality.

The question for one product and one person:

Who benefits, who bears the costs, through which systems, compared with what alternative — and what must change?

Each impact is scored on its own.

`priority = 100 × (magnitude × likelihood) / 16`

Magnitude is 0–4. Likelihood is 1–4, within a named horizon. The result is a queue score. It is not a probability and not a measure of welfare. Benefits and harms are never subtracted. Headcount is recorded and not multiplied. A blank rating stays blank. A harm at magnitude 3 or 4 goes to independent review no matter how large the benefits are.

The hypothetical sheet in the app is an illustration, not evidence.
